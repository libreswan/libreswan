IKEv2 optimized rekey (draft-ietf-ipsecme-ikev2-sa-ts-payloads-opt) of a Child
SA that narrowed its traffic selectors.

road (behind NAT) has leftsubnet=0.0.0.0/0, leftmodecfgclient=yes and
narrowing=yes; east assigns it an address from a pool.  With
child-sa-pfs-info-supported=yes the Child SA rekeys are optimized, so no TS
payloads are sent; the rekeyed Child SA has to keep exactly the same, narrowed,
traffic selectors.

The rekey is initiated from both sides: road, with the single IP, rekeys twice
(02-road-rekey.sh); then east, the server with the pool, initiates a rekey
(03-east-rekey.sh) which road, as the responder, handles (04-road-check.sh).
