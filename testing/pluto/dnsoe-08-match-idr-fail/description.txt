dns-match-id fails on the initiator: the A record for IDr does not match
the IKE source address of the responder.

East authenticates as @east-private.testing.libreswan.org using its own
key, which matches the IPSECKEY published for that name.  The A record
for east-private is 192.0.2.254 but east's IKE source address is
192.1.2.23.

West has dns-match-id=yes and rightrsasigkey=%dnsondemand.  It fetches
the IPSECKEY and the A record for the IDr, the A record check fails,
and west gives up by deleting the IKE SA.

West tries twice.

The first time the DNS queries are sent to the nameserver when
IKE_SA_INIT is sent and the answers arrive in the background.

The second time west has the DNS answers cached so the callbacks run
before the queries return.  Pluto must survive both.
