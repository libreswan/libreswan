OE/IDNULL with CAT, NAT'd and non-NAT'd hosts.
This is a realistic setup of an e-mail client and servers running OE.
All hosts use the same ipsec.conf and ikev2-oe.conf.

- road is NAT'd towards east and west (a laptop sending e-mail to the servers).
- east and west lease an address from their pool to road.
- road is not NAT'd towards north (an e-mail server on its LAN).
- north and west are not NAT'd (e-mail servers talking to each other).
- north has no OE policy for east, so there is no north-east tunnel.

north, west and road ping their peers with fping --lossy 15 to trigger OE.
final.sh checks that every tunnel carried traffic, not the exact byte or
packet counts.
