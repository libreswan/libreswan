IKEv2 optimized rekey (draft-ietf-ipsecme-ikev2-sa-ts-payloads-opt) combined
with child-sa-pfs-info-supported=yes: the optimized rekey is negotiated and,
as the KE method was negotiated in IKE_AUTH, the first rekey could be optimized.

After the tunnel is up, the initiator (west) uses the impairment
disable_optimized_rekey and rekeys.  The traditional rekey, with SA and TS
payloads, has to be used.
