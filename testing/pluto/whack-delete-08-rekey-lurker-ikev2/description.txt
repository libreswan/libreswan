Rekey then lurk, then delete, a connection

Code terminating IKE SA needs to be careful that the next in order
isn't also deleted, causing it to step into the use-after-free Abyss.

- establish a permanent connection

  NEW2OLD order is Child SA -> IKE SA

- rekey IKE SA

  NEW2OLD order is IKE SA -> Child SA

- lurk the existing connection

  On peer kill pluto, and then bring up connection leaving existing
  connection lurking

  NEW2OLD order is IKE SA -> Child SA -> Lurking IKE SA -> Lurking Child SA

- delete connection

See #3133 pluto SIGSEGV in next_state() (use-after-free) when deleting
a connection that owns a lingering IKE SA with an older Child SA
(Dmitrii Krasnov).
