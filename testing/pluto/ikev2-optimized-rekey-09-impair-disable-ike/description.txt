IKEv2 optimized rekey (draft-ietf-ipsecme-ikev2-sa-ts-payloads-opt) combined
with child-sa-pfs-info-supported=yes: the optimized rekey is negotiated and,
as the KE method was negotiated in IKE_AUTH, the first rekey could be optimized.

The initiator (west) first uses the impairment disable_optimized_rekey and
performs a regular Child SA rekey and a regular IKE SA rekey.  It then removes
the impairment and performs an optimized Child SA rekey and an optimized IKE SA
rekey.
