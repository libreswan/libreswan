IKEv2 Mismatched authentication on the initiator (west), and on the responder.

Initiator is configured as
leftauthby=null 	leftid=%null
rightauth=rsasig 	rightid="%fromcert"

responder is configured with symmetric auth
authby=null
leftid="%null"
rightid="%fromcert"

This test should not establish a connection. The initiator should reject it.

The initiator sends AUTHENTICATION_FAILED in an encrypted INFORMATIONAL
request. The responder deletes the IKE and Child SAs without reviving
the connection, even with auto=keep.
