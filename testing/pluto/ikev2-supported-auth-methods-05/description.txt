RFC 9593 test. West has authby=secret and east has authby=ecdsa.
West upon receiving the SUPPORTED_AUTH_METHODS notification from east,
since they have no methods in common, the negotiated authby results empty.
West should fall back to its locally configured authby=secret and
authenticate using PSK.
The connection then fails cleanly: east rejects west's AUTH
because east's policy requires ECDSA from the peer.
